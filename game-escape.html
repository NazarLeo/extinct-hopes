One goal: get out aliveFile 01-A

The exit is somewhere above you, behind locked doors and dead power. Search the rooms, find what opens the next section, and keep moving. The longer you stay in one place, the more chances it has to find you.

The hybridFile 01-B

It is not a scripted jump scare on a timer. It moves through the facility on its own schedule, and it adapts to where you keep going. Hiding works — but only if you picked the spot before it got there.

A story worth finishingFile 01-C

Full voice acting with subtitles, notes and recordings left behind by the people who worked here, and an answer to what your father actually saw. Cinematic story sequences sit between the sections you have to survive.

FeaturesFile 01-D
